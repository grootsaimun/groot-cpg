Features

Everything this cover page generator can do, in simple words.

4 cover types

Pick Lab Report, Assignment, Group Project or Lab Index at the top of the form. Each type shows only the fields it needs.

Example: Choose Lab Report for your Experiment 5 cover, then switch to Lab Index for the list page of all experiments.

Ready-made designs

8 designs for Lab Report, 8 for Assignment, 7 for Group Project and 3 for Lab Index. Tap a small preview to use it. You can change it any time.

Live preview

The cover updates as you type. On wide screens it stays beside the form. On a phone, use the Details and Preview tabs to switch, or use the small live preview inside the form. A quick paper size box sits next to the preview.

Zoom and fullscreen

Use + and − to zoom the preview from 50% to 400%, or ↻ to go back to 100%. On a phone you can also pinch with two fingers. The Fullscreen button opens a big view, and Close (or the Esc key) returns.

Simple details form

For Lab Report and Assignment, fill in cover heading, optional group, course title and code, experiment or assignment number and title, submission date, teacher name, designation, institution, department, and your own name, ID, section and semester.

Example: Press Today to put today's date. For semester, type your own (up to 12 letters) or pick Fourth from the list. Departments are chosen from a list.

Group Project covers

Add 1 to 6 members, each with a name and an ID, together with the group name or number, project title, course, section, semester, teacher and date.

Example: Set members to 3 and only three name and ID rows are shown on the cover.

Lab Index maker

Choose 1 to 30 rows and keep 2 to 8 table columns. The starting columns are Sl no, Date, Experiment Name, Page and Signature; you can rename, add or remove them. Type each experiment's name, date and page number, or leave a row empty to write it by hand after printing.

Example: Type 5 experiments now, keep the other rows empty, print, and fill the rest with a pen later.

Header, logo and remarks

Change the university and department names, show or hide the logo, or upload your own logo. For Lab Report and Assignment you can also turn the remarks box and ruled lines on or off.

Colors

15 ready-made color themes such as Navy & Teal, Maroon & Gold and White & Forest Green. You can then fine-tune the main, accent, text, line and page background colors, or pick a quick background: White, Ivory, Sand, Paper gray, Sky, Mint or Blush.

Example: Pick Classic Black if you will print in black and white.

Paper size and font

Paper: A3, A4, A5, B5, US Letter, US Legal, Executive or Tabloid. Cover font: Caladea, Inter, Georgia, Arial or Noto Sans Bengali.

Download as PDF or JPG

PDF is best for printing and submitting. JPG is a sharp, high-resolution image that is handy for sharing in chats or uploading to a portal. The file name is made for you from your course code, experiment or assignment number and your ID. Group Project files use the course code and group number, and Lab Index files use the course code.

Attach your own PDF

Upload your finished report or assignment (PDF, up to 20 MB). The cover becomes its first page, and you download one single PDF.

Example: Upload my-report.pdf, press Download PDF, and get cover + report together.

Save on this device

Your details are remembered automatically in this browser. You can also save a full cover, or save only a design (template, colors, font, paper, logo and remarks settings) and reuse it later. Open selected loads a save, and Delete selected asks you to click twice so nothing is removed by mistake. A Duplicate button makes a copy of the current cover.

Example: Save your style as My lab design. Next week, open it and type only the new experiment name and number.

Backup and clean-up

Open Settings to see how many covers and designs you have saved and to download a backup file (.json) as a safe copy. You can also delete all saved covers and designs, or reset everything. This page cannot import a backup file back yet.

Themes

Open Theme from the menu and choose Light, Cream light, White & forest green, Dark, or Device default, which follows your phone or computer automatically.

Animations

In Theme → Animations you can turn on Reduce motion to stop most movement, or turn on Falling leaves to see a few leaves drift down the page. Falling leaves is off by default.

Reset to defaults

Clears the form and brings back the starting design with one tap. It asks you to click twice to confirm, and your saved covers and designs are kept.

Safe text check

English and Bengali both work on the cover. If you type letters from other scripts that the cover may not draw, a yellow warning appears so you can check the preview before printing.

Theme

Turns off most movement, including the animated title.

Off by default. Turn it on to see a few leaves drift down the page.

Guidelines and Policies

  • English and Bengali both work. Bengali text needs an internet connection the first time so its font can load. Other scripts and special symbols may not appear on the cover, and a warning shows if something is not supported.
  • Write names exactly as in university records. Check your ID, section and course code twice.
  • Keep titles short. Very long text is shrunk to fit, so shorter titles look cleaner.
  • Semester can be up to 12 letters. Pick from the list or type your own.
  • Check the preview for spelling and date before you download.
  • Attached PDF must be a PDF file of 20 MB or less.
  • Group Project covers can list 1 to 6 members. Lab Index covers can have 1 to 30 rows and 2 to 8 columns.
  • Follow your teacher's rules. If your department asks for a special cover format, use that as the final guide.
  • Paper: A4 is the default. Choose another size only if that is the paper you will print on.
  • Scale: In the print window choose Actual size / 100%. Turn off "Fit to page" and "Shrink to fit".
  • Headers and footers: Turn off the browser's date and web address lines.
  • Best file: Print from the PDF. Use JPG for sharing only.
  • Backgrounds: White or light backgrounds print best and save ink. Dark colors may use a lot of ink.
  • Black and white printers: Colors turn gray. The Classic Black color theme gives the cleanest result.
  • Test first: Print one page before printing many copies.
